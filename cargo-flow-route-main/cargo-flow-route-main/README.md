--------------------------------------------------

PRODUCT CONTEXT

--------------------------------------------------

CargoFlow is a B2B cargo reservation and transport platform.

The concept:

Public transport buses already travel between cities and towns every day.

A significant amount of useful cargo capacity may remain unused.

CargoFlow allows courier/logistics companies to reserve that available capacity.

Instead of sending a dedicated delivery vehicle for every shipment:

Courier

    ↓

CargoFlow

    ↓

Available bus capacity

    ↓

Scheduled bus route

    ↓

Destination

    ↓

Local delivery / handoff

The initial geographic context is Maharashtra, India.

The initial data is based on Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC) timetable information.

The MVP focuses on selected Maharashtra divisions and routes.

Do NOT claim that CargoFlow is officially operated by MSRTC.

Present it as a technology platform / proposed infrastructure model that could be operated or adopted by a public transport organization.
